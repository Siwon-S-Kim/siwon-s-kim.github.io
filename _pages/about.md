I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Research Institute of Basic Sciences, Seoul National
University. I received my Ph.D. in Mathematical Sciences from Seoul National University in
2026, under the supervision of Woong Kook, after a double major in physics and mathematics
at Konkuk University.

I am an engineering researcher with a physics and mathematics background who works at the
intersection of data science and AI engineering, with a focus on practical impact. Most of my
applied work is in electrocardiogram (ECG) analysis, including delineation models,
arrhythmia classification, and rule-based diagnostic software built with industry and clinical
partners. My recent research interests are self-supervised learning and hierarchical
representations for multivariate time series, as well as outlier detection on transformer
representation spaces.

You can find my [publications](/publications/), [talks](/talks/) and [teaching](/teaching/)
here, and a full account of my work — including industry projects — in my
[CV](/files/CV.pdf).
